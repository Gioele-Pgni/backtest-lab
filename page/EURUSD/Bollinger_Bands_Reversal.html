EURUSD Bollinger Bands Reversal

Operational Logic & Signal Confirmation The model implements a statistical mean-reversion architecture engineered on a 30-minute operational time horizon. Designed to capture short-term price adjustments following extreme market expansions, the system utilizes customized Bollinger Bands not merely as dynamic threshold indicators, but as a multi-stage execution filter. Capital commitment requires explicit structural confirmation: the asset must first extend beyond the outer band boundaries to establish a state of statistical overextension, followed by a confirmed candle close back inside the volatility bands. This sequential logic ensures that entry occurs only when counter-trend momentum has effectively initiated, filtering out false breakouts during strong directional regimes. Execution Framework & Risk Parameters Position management relies on a continuous operational protocol featuring active reverse-on-signal mechanics during market hours, enabling the model to adapt dynamically to shifting intraday liquidity.To mitigate systemic tail risk and overnight weekend exposure, the strategy incorporates a mandatory temporal constraint: all active positions are systematically liquidated—or reversed depending on state parameters—at the Friday session close. This hard-coded exit rule ensures total insulation from weekend gap risk, prioritizing capital preservation across weekly cycles.

Preliminary Performance & Model Validation Initial empirical testing of the unoptimized model confirms the underlying validity of the core mean-reverting thesis. The benchmark equity curve demonstrates a resilient long-term upward trajectory—generating a theoretical cumulative gain exceeding $80,000 since 2010—driven by consistent performance run-ups across distinct market regimes. While the raw signal architecture exhibits periodic drawdown friction during extended directional trends, the baseline statistical edge remains well-defined. Optimization & Risk Management Roadmap To refine structural stability and smooth capital drawdowns, the research protocol now advances to a structured multi-stage optimization framework: Light Parameter Refinement: Conducting targeted parameter sensitivity analysis on the 30-minute sampling resolution to eliminate structural inefficiencies without inducing overfitting risks. Dynamic Risk Control Integration: Implementing specialized position-sizing algorithms and stop-loss/take-profit boundaries designed to compress maximum drawdown metrics. Multi-Step Evaluation Protocol: Systematically auditing trade-offs between net profitability and risk-adjusted metrics (Sharpe/Sortino) at each calibration stage prior to final Out-of-Sample (OOS) validation.
