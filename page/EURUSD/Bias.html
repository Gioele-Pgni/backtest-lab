EURUSD Weekly Bias Strategy

Operational Logic & Weekly Bias Capture
The strategy implements a seasonal time-based model engineered to capture structural micro-trends across the weekly session cycle. Operating on a 60-minute timeframe, the core algorithm exploits directional asymmetries by applying distinct holding constraints: Long positions are held across a 2-session operational window, whereas Short positions are restricted to a single-session horizon to align with underlying currency flows.

Structural Challenges & Instrument Dynamics
Capturing weekly directional bias on EURUSD presents significant operational friction. A complex combination of evolving market conditions, structural shifts, and macroeconomic factors has made an algorithmic approach particularly challenging on this asset. This structural complexity requires strict temporal execution rules to ensure capital is committed only during high-probability bias windows.

Optimization Protocols & Overfitting Mitigation
Extensive parameter tuning and structural refinement protocols were essential to develop a robust, acceptable system from this raw bias thesis. Given the inherent sensitivity of seasonal time-based models on currency pairs, the primary challenge during engineering was strictly avoiding overfitting—ensuring that parameters capture genuine statistical tendencies rather than curve-fitting past noise.

Portfolio Integration & Structural Uncertainty
While this strategy carries a higher degree of structural uncertainty compared to other algorithmic models, its inclusion within a broader, multi-strategy quantitative portfolio contributes meaningfully to overall operational stability, smoothing out collective equity line volatility.

Optimization Outcomes & Risk Architecture
The light calibration phase yielded robust performance metrics across the multi-year dataset while validating the foundational logic. To accommodate the structural volatility required by a mean-reverting architecture on a 30-minute sampling resolution, the risk control framework employs a wider protective stop set at 80 pips.

This calibrated distance grants the underlying asset necessary market movement, specifically mitigating exposure to short-term liquidity sweeps and predatory stop-hunting dynamics typical of mean-reversion entries. Profit capture remains governed by signal-based exit dynamics and the mandatory end-of-week liquidation, intentionally omitting explicit fixed take-profit targets to allow full market capture during strong mean-reverting expansions.

Drawdown Profile & Model Resilience
Although the strategy exhibits localized drawdown expansion during adverse regime shifts, its recovery profile displays exceptional structural resilience. The equity curve consistently exhibits rapid, "V-shaped" momentum recoveries following peak-to-trough impairments, confirming that the model dynamically realigns with market mechanics rather than suffering structural decay.

Consecutive Win Sequences & Edge Persistence
Empirical evaluation of performance clustering reveals that profits materialize in distinct, high-conviction sequences. While localized drawdown phases occur during unfavorable volatility regimes, the onset of a favorable statistical window triggers immediate, sustained profitability. This operational behavior confirms that when the market realigns with the core thesis, the strategy's edge operates with high density and consistency, systematically reclaiming prior peak-to-trough losses.

Statistical Recovery vs. Structural Decay
This pattern of consecutive win clusters highlights a crucial distinction in the strategy’s risk-return profile:

• Regime Adaptation: The model’s drawdowns are non-structural—they reflect temporary misalignment with market conditions rather than fundamental system failure.
• Capital Velocity: The rapid execution of winning trades during active regimes provides the necessary capital velocity to compress the temporal duration of equity drawdowns.
• Asymmetric Recovery Rate: The high win-rate concentration during favorable windows offsets the historical friction of the 80-pip protective stop, validating the trade location logic.

Portfolio Allocation & Risk-Adjusted Sizing Impact
This practical demonstration highlights the strategic value of dynamic portfolio management protocols. By accepting a minor trade-off in raw net profitability, the framework achieves a substantial compression in maximum drawdown—significantly improving the strategy's operational sustainability and psychological tolerance.

Final Evaluation

Capital Compounding & Position Sizing Dynamics
To demonstrate the architecture's compounding potential, a conservative percentage-of-equity position sizing model was applied to the baseline system. This non-linear scaling framework produced significant capital expansion over the backtest horizon:

• Cumulative Net Profit: $857,000
• Maximum Peak-to-Trough Drawdown: $250,000

While absolute dollar drawdown expands alongside portfolio growth, the statistical edge efficiently absorbs leverage without causing structural account decay.

Roadmap: Out-of-Sample Validation & Deployment
With baseline development and risk architecture finalized, the strategy enters its final deployment pipeline:
