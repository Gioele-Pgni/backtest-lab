EURUSD Weekly Bias Strategy

Operational Logic & Weekly Bias Capture
The strategy implements a seasonal time-based model engineered to capture structural micro-trends across the weekly session cycle. Operating on a 60-minute timeframe, the core algorithm exploits directional asymmetries by applying distinct holding constraints: Long positions are held across a 2-session operational window, whereas Short positions are restricted to a single-session horizon to align with underlying currency flows.

Structural Challenges & Instrument Dynamics
Capturing weekly directional bias on EURUSD presents significant operational friction. A complex combination of evolving market conditions, structural shifts, and macroeconomic factors has made an algorithmic approach particularly challenging on this asset. This structural complexity requires strict temporal execution rules to ensure capital is committed only during high-probability bias windows.

Optimization Protocols & Overfitting Mitigation
Extensive parameter tuning and structural refinement protocols were essential to develop a robust, acceptable system from this raw bias thesis. Given the inherent sensitivity of seasonal time-based models on currency pairs, the primary challenge during engineering was strictly avoiding overfitting—ensuring that parameters capture genuine statistical tendencies rather than curve-fitting past noise.

Portfolio Integration & Structural Uncertainty
While this strategy carries a higher degree of structural uncertainty compared to other algorithmic models, its inclusion within a broader, multi-strategy quantitative portfolio contributes meaningfully to overall operational stability, smoothing out collective equity line volatility.

Final Evaluation: EURUSD Bias

Despite the inherent complexities of the strategy and the underlying market dynamics, the model presents an acceptable performance outlook.
